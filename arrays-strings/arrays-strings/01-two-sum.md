## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)
**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach
Nested loop checking every pair against the target — brute force, simple to reason about for small inputs.

### Complexity
- Time: O(n^2)
- Space: O(1) excluding output

### Notes
A hash map would bring this to O(n) time — worth trying next iteration.
